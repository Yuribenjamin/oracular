**Learning no longer drifts without outcomes.** The outcome rates a signal is judged by, with it and without it, were Laplace-smoothed, which centres both on 0.5 with one pseudo-hit each: two groups with no outcome at all then differ only by their sizes, so with zero outcomes a common signal drifted negative and a rare one positive, and a weight could change sign on no evidence (a weight of 2 seen in 90 of 100 outcome-free episodes ended at −0.59). Both rates are now shrunk toward the intent's own base rate by two pseudo-episodes, `(hits + 2·q₀) / (n + 2)` with `q₀ = outcomes / episodes`: a signal whose outcome rate equals the base rate learns exactly zero whatever the sample sizes, with no outcome every rate is 0 and no weight moves away from the developer's, and a weight never changes sign without outcomes on at least one side. The association-rule baseline becomes the base rate itself. `tune` fits with the same smoothing, so its weights, intervals and lifts move a little on small samples (a signal seen once with the outcome over a low base rate is worth a little less than before); on large samples the change is in the third decimal. Every learned number in the documentation's generated tables was regenerated. The state format is unchanged, and existing learned counts are kept: only the weight read from them moves.
