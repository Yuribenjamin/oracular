**Exact lower bound.** `RevealOptions.bound: 'exact'` makes `lower` the Beta distribution's own quantile at the one-sided level `z` names (Φ(−z), so 1.28 is still a 90% bound) instead of the normal approximation p − z·sd, which is optimistic exactly where the gate matters: thin evidence near the threshold. Beta(4, 3) puts p at 0.571, the approximation at 0.347 and the exact tenth percentile at 0.333; by Beta(30, 20) the two agree to a thousandth. `predict`, `shouldReveal`, `evaluate`, the recorder and `evidenceToReveal` all follow the option, resolved per call → intent → engine → default like the others, and the config schema lists it. Nothing moves unless it is set: every state and every number without `bound` is exactly what it was. Under `'exact'`, `z: 0` is the median rather than the mean, and `lower` can sit above `p` for a prior near 1 with little strength. A stored config that sets `bound` is read by 0.8 and later; 0.7 throws a `TypeError` on the unknown key, so drop the option before rolling a deploy back. The state version is unchanged. The quantile costs 0.54 kB: the core goes from 6.65 kB to 7.19 kB minified and gzipped, past the 7 kB ceiling the stability page set, so the ceiling moves once, to 7.9 kB (the shipped size plus about 10%), and the quickstart budget from 3.9 kB to 4.5 kB; the stability page records the decision.
