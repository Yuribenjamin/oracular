**An episode per outcome, for intents shaped as attempts.** The episode is the session, shared by every intent, so for an upload that fails, completes, and completes again in one session the engine counted one episode: the second completion, with nothing seen before it, was a duplicate under 0.10's rule, and a second attempt that failed and never completed stayed neutral at the close. The failure signal never saw an outcome without it, which is the contrast learning needs, and `tune` printed "1 counted episode" where the app saw two. `IntentConfig.episodePerOutcome: true` makes each outcome an episode of its own for that intent: a later outcome in the same session counts as another episode even with nothing seen since, and signals seen after an outcome take a miss at the session's close, or wait under `attribution`. Everything else is as before, `resetOnOutcome` is independent, and `tune` follows the engine's counts. Off unless set: every state and every number without it is exactly what it was. With it set, a duplicated outcome event counts twice, so dedupe such events in the app for these intents. The JSON Schema lists it. Found by the second production integration, whose workaround, `endEpisode` on the outcome, ends the session for every other intent too.

**Rollback.** A stored config that sets `episodePerOutcome` loads in 0.13 and later, which do not read the stored snapshot when the app passes its config; 0.12 and earlier throw a `TypeError` on the unknown key, so remove it from the saved config before rolling a deploy back that far. The state version is unchanged.
